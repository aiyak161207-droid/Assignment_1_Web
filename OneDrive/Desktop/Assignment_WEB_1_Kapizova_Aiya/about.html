The World, Where Everything Is Connected

Pandora

Pandora is a habitable planet in the Avatar universe. Its forests, oceans, and mountains are filled with unusual animals and plants. The Na'vi perceive nature as a large living network connected to Eywa. This is why the film is captivating, especially with its visual stylization. Pandora's visual language unites science and mythology.

Key Dates

  1. December 18, 2009 - premiere of the first film.
  2. March 7, 2010 - the film received three Academy Awards.
  3. December 16, 2022 - «The Way of Water» was released.
  4. December 19, 2025 - «Fire and Ash» was released.

Key Facts About Pandora

  • The action takes place on Pandora's moon.
  • The Na'vi are the indigenous people of Pandora.
  • Tsahik connects the Na'vi to the animals.
  • Bioluminescence is particularly noticeable at night.

Character

Neitiiri

Neitiiri - a warrior of the Omaticaya clan and a guide for Jake Sully in the world of Pandora. Her view of nature helps the audience see that connection is more important than strength. She remains a brave and loyal defender of her family and home.

«Everything is connected. The same energy flows through everything alive».
